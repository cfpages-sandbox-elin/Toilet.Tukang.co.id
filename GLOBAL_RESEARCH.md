- RSVP total, venue marketing capacity, package price, portfolio, prior event, or follower count cannot prove the actual layout, zone capacity, facilities, vendor capacity, safety, rights, or delivered scope.

- Privacy/consent, copyright/licence, performer/music/broadcast rights, ownership of deliverables, portfolio use, and platform terms are separate.
- Use current BMKG warnings plus structure/equipment/site-specific criteria; seasonal assumptions or one weather icon are not decision controls.
